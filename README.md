# ♻️ Ruta PET Protocol

> **Infrastructure for PET Plastic Traceability & Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) Compliance in LATAM**

## 📌 Overview

**Ruta PET** (by Circular Polymer Labs LLC) is a ReFi & IoT protocol designed to bring radical transparency to post-consumer plastic recovery in Latin America. 

By combining BLE-enabled scales, mobile geofencing, and immutable smart contracts, Ruta PET enables consumer goods corporations to fulfill legal EPR requirements (Resolution 1407/2018) while executing direct micropayouts to informal waste collectors.

## ⚙️ Core Architecture

- **IoT Data Capture:** BLE Smart Scales + GPS Geofencing
- **Proof of Collection (PoC):** Verifiable telemetry and timestamps
- **Tokenomics:** 1 RPET Token = 1,000 kg (1 Metric Ton) of recycled PET

## 🛠 Tech Stack

- **Hardware:** BLE 4.0/5.0 Digital Scales
- **Mobile Client:** React Native / Android App
- **Blockchain:** EVM (Polygon / Arbitrum), Solana, Stellar/Soroban
- **Database:** PostgreSQL for Enterprise ESG Audits

⚡ Dual-Engine Architecture (Impact & Traceability)
To address both environmental additionality and enterprise compliance, Ruta PET operates on a Dual-Engine Model:

Level 1: RutaPET Prime (Direct Recovery & Pure Additionality)

Level 2: RutaPET Standard (Third-Party Aggregator Traceability)

🟩 Level 1: RutaPET Prime (Direct Recovery & Pure Additionality)
Operation: Executed directly via Circular Polymer Labs' fleet of ECO-PET cargo tricycles and dedicated collection routes in targeted territories (pilot in Sucre/Sincelejo, Colombia).

Core Value: Pure Environmental Additionality. Rescues post-consumer PET that would otherwise end up in landfills, waterways, or open dumpsites.

Impact: Generates premium plastic credits, provides immediate zero-fee micropayouts directly to informal collectors, and offers high-impact ESG assets for global Web3/ReFi backers.

🟦 Level 2: RutaPET Standard (SaaS & Third-Party Traceability)
Operation: Licensed as an open-source technology stack (App + BLE Scale IoT) for existing recycling warehouses (bodegas de reciclaje) and waste management cooperatives.

Core Value: Anti-Fraud & Legal Certainty. Eliminates double-counting, altered weighing logs, and fraudulent invoicing.

Impact: Provides consumer goods corporations (CPGs) with immutable, audit-ready compliance certificates required by national environmental regulations (e.g., Colombia Res. 1407/2018).
